# About Me

<img src="assets/img/profile.jpg" alt="Profile Image" width="250" align="right">

I’m **Maci Kesler**, an advanced graduate researcher in nuclear physics, affiliated with Kent State University and active in ePIC, JLab, SDCC, and STAR collaborations.  

My work focuses on modular, reproducible workflows for physics analysis, mentoring students, and designing scalable solutions for complex environments.  

This page gives a quick overview of my background, research interests, and collaborations.
