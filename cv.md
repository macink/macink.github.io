## Research
- Specialize in nuclear imaging through diffractive scattering and exclusive vector meson production:
    - developing simulation models
    - integrating results into data analysis
    - apply resolution conditioned machine learning methods
- Contributing to early science papers and analysis notes within ePIC, including the first published analysis note
- Mentoring students in machine learning and physics motivated analysis approaches

## Recent Publications
- Early Science Paper, ePIC Collaboration (2026): contributor to one of the first physics papers from ePIC
- Pre‑Technical Design Report, ePIC Collaboration (2025): contributor to detector performance and physics opportunities study
- Kesler et al. (2025): proposed technique for resolving diffractive patterns in form factors (toy model study)
- Analysis Note, ePIC Collaboration (2025): lead author of the first official ePIC analysis note
